Methodology & formulas
Combustion CO₂ (per fuel) = as-fed fuel mass × carbon %(as-fired) × 44/12 × oxidation factor (OF). Every fuel property is entered on an as-fired basis (carbon, H, O, S, ash and LHV), matching the as-fed tonnage and the way the plant laboratory reports them, so no moisture correction is applied to CO₂ — fuel moisture is used only in the mass and water balance (dry fuel mass = as-fed mass × (1 − moisture %); the moisture leaves as water vapour). Lab results reported on a dry basis are converted before entry with Xar = Xdry × (1 − M) for elemental and ash fractions and LHVar = LHVdry × (1 − M) − 2.443 × M (MJ/kg, M = wet-basis moisture fraction; 2.443 MJ/kg is the latent heat of water at 25 °C) — the Convert dry-basis lab data helper under the fuel table does this. This is pure IPCC mass/carbon-balance accounting — LHV plays no part in this formula. LHV is used only for the separate Thermal energy metric below (a heat-input memo figure), never to derive CO₂. Fossil/biogenic split follows each fuel's biogenic carbon share — set manually via the Type column (Fossil / Biogenic / Mixed) rather than inferred, since supplier data rarely states it rigorously; choosing "Mixed" unlocks a custom biogenic-share % for blended streams (e.g. RDF).
Calcination CO₂ — two independent methods, cross-checked: Method I (clinker XRF stoichiometry) = clinker mass × (%CaO × 44.01/56.08 + %MgO × 44.01/40.30) × CFckd (see the CKD correction paragraph below), using %CaO/%MgO as measured on the clinker — the same basis as the plant's own SKU-allocation sheet. Method II (mass-balance difference) = dry raw meal + retained fuel ash − clinker − bypass dust — whatever solid mass didn't come out as clinker or dust is assumed to have left as calcination CO₂ (this reproduces the plant workbook's own mass-balance calcination figure). The Calcination CO₂ — Dual-Method Cross-Check card shows both, live, with the %-delta between them as a convergence check; Method I feeds every GWP figure below, since it doesn't depend on the mass balance closing. The delta is graded against a set tolerance (default ±5% = Converged, up to twice that = Check assumptions, beyond = Diverging; editable in Mass balance assumptions). A large or growing delta between the two is itself a diagnostic — it means the mass balance (raw meal in, clinker out, dust/bypass losses) and the clinker chemistry assay disagree about how much carbonate actually decomposed.
CKD correction (CFckd) — Method I is IPCC's Tier 2 form, Mcl × EFcl × CFckd, where EFcl comes from this plant's own clinker assay and CFckd = 1 + (Md/Mcl) × Cd × Fd × (EFc/EFcl) (IPCC 2006 Vol. 3 Eq. 2.5; EFc = 0.43971 t CO₂/t CaCO₃, Table 2.1). Md is cement kiln dust not recycled to the kiln; Cd is its original carbonate fraction (defaults to the raw mix's, which IPCC allows); Fd is how much of that carbonate was already calcined. For this plant, operators confirmed on 2026-10-14 that CKD is recycled in a closed loop to kiln feed with no export and no bypass, so Md = 0 and CFckd = 1.000 — a verified value, not an omitted term. Sites that sell or send CKD off-site enter only the amount (t or kg), where it was measured (weighed at dispatch, fed to the cement mill, or captured at the dust filter with the returned part subtracted), and optionally a lab CO₂ analysis of the dust in the CKD Correction card. The console calculates Md, Cd (from the raw mix) and Fd (from the dust assay, or a conservative 1.0 without one) and then the factor; a measured amount also replaces the bypass-dust % in Method II, and the dual-method comparison then uses Method I after CFckd so both describe all CO₂ released in the kiln. Allocation: exported CKD is treated with a cut-off approach — the kiln's CO₂ stays in this plant's Scope 1 and is allocated entirely to clinker/cement, and the CKD leaves the gate with no allocated burden. This is a provisional methodological choice; it should be revisited if CKD ever becomes a revenue-bearing co-product.
Thermal energy = Σ (as-fed fuel mass × LHV), reported as GJ and as specific heat consumption (MJ / t clinker) — independent of the mass-based CO₂ calc above.
Mass balance: Inputs = dry raw meal + raw-meal moisture + dry fuel + fuel moisture + combustion air (Σ as-fed fuel × stoichiometric air ratio × excess-air factor; the air ratio is calculated per kg of fuel as fired from each fuel's as-fired C, H, O, S as 11.51·C + 34.34·(H − O/8) + 4.31·S, and the plant's three original fuels keep the plant's own constants) + process water. Outputs = clinker + calcination CO₂ + combustion CO₂ + water vapour released + water discharged + fuel ash to dust/bypass + raw-meal bypass dust + unreacted air (air − O₂ consumed, O₂ ≈ combustion CO₂ × 32/44). Closure = (In − Out) / In. Bypass-dust loss defaults to 0% (the plant runs 100% closed-loop dust recycling) and quench-water evaporation defaults to 100% (the plant's water balance closes at exactly 0 on that assumption).
SKU allocation: Step 1 divides total Scope-1 pyroprocess CO₂ (calcination + combustion, fossil-reported) by clinker production. Step 2 multiplies by the selected clinker-to-cement factor. The SKU list is the plant's product portfolio; product-specific clinker-to-cement ratios are not yet available, so all default to 0.92 — edit per product.
Scope 2 (Electricity) = electricity (burning + quench) × grid emission factor, shown in its own card with a per-t-clinker figure. Reported separately and not included in the Scope-1 pyroprocess totals above, to avoid mixing emission scopes. The grid factor is not in the plant's source workbook; the grid factor is sourced from the plant's own SimaPro export (see Background Parameters & Emission Factors panel).
Upstream (Scope 3) = Σ (as-fed fuel mass × its background/upstream EF), set in the Background Parameters & Emission Factors panel. These are well-to-gate (extraction, processing, transport) factors for purchased fuels — not combustion or calcination — and are kept out of the Scope-1 and Scope-2 figures above to avoid double counting. None of these EFs are in the plant's source workbook; Petcoke defaults to 600.00 kg CO₂e/t and CKB to 679.38 kg CO₂e/t, both SimaPro exports — CKB's is a Heavy Fuel Oil proxy (no direct cracker-bottom dataset exists), so treat it as an approximation. Rice Husk defaults to 0 as a Strict Audit Mode placeholder until you enter a supplier or database (e.g. ecoinvent, DEFRA) figure, which also matches biomass-LCA convention treating agricultural residue/waste with no dedicated cultivation burden as ~0 upstream. Raw Meal is deliberately excluded from this upstream calculation and locked at 0 in the panel — it's an on-site intermediate (crushed/ground from the plant's own limestone), not an external purchase, and its chemical footprint is already accounted for via the kiln calcination formula (CaCO₃ decomposition) and the raw mill's electricity draw. Treating it as an upstream purchase would create a system-boundary overlap and double-count that footprint.
Electricity's grid emission factor (Scope 2) is set in the Background Parameters & Emission Factors panel, alongside the upstream EFs, since both are background constants rather than period-specific operational data.
Validated against the plant workbook: for Jan–Jun, this console's combustion CO₂ (fossil 34,012 t / biogenic 1,965 t) and Method II calcination CO₂ (31,771 t) reproduce the workbook's own figures exactly — confirming carbon content, fuel mass, molecular-weight factors and the raw-meal/clinker mass balance all match. Over the same period Method I (clinker XRF, 537 kg/t) and Method II (mass balance, 552 kg/t) agree within ~2.8%, consistent with the workbook's overall mass balance closing to within 0.09%. The oxidation factor defaults to 1.0 (full oxidation): that is the IPCC 2006 Vol. 2 default for every fuel (Table 1.4), and the WBCSD/CSI Cement CO₂ Protocol treats all kiln fuel carbon as fully oxidised because kiln temperatures make unburnt carbon negligible. Change it only with a measured, plant-specific value. All fuel properties are on the as-fired basis that matches the reported tonnage (the plant's source calculation applies no dry-basis correction), and fuel moisture affects only the mass and water balance; it is 0% for the three plant fuels until a measured value is entered.
Production, fuel tonnage and %CaO/%MgO are the plant's actual Jan–Jul 2026 figures; electricity and process water are actual through Jun (Jul shows 0 — not yet metered in the source). Aug–Dec are 0 pending reporting, except %CaO/%MgO and fuel LHV, which the plant workbook already carries for the full year. Grid emission factor is not in this workbook, but is sourced from a separate SimaPro export (see Background Parameters & Emission Factors panel) rather than a generic placeholder.
Import Monthly Data (top of the left column) reads an uploaded .xlsx client-side (SheetJS — nothing is uploaded to a server) and replaces the baseline above with fresh figures from its "mass balance matrix" sheet: Raw Mill (row 6), inbound moisture (row 7), Pet Coke/AF (Rice Husk)/CKB mass (rows 9–11, cols E–P) and LHV (same rows, cols S–AD), Electricity (row 12), Water (row 14), Clinker Production (row 16) and %CaO/%MgO (rows 16–17, cols S–AD) — the same cells this console's own baseline data came from. It does not touch fuel composition (carbon %, ash %, air ratio), upstream EFs, or assumptions, since those aren't tracked monthly in the source file. The Template layout reference (same card) documents the exact sheet name, row labels and column layout for building or checking your own import file. No ready-to-fill .xlsx template is available yet; the import is a demo of the import path.
Calculation Trace & Audit Note — the small monospace line under Combustion CO₂, the active Calcination CO₂ method, Upstream (Scope 3) and Final SKU Intensity is generated live from the current inputs and state (which calcination method is toggled on, which upstream EFs are non-zero, the active SKU profile) — it names the exact formula and the numbers plugged into it for this period, so a figure can be audited without leaving the card it's shown in.
Mass Balance — Block Flow Diagram (above the Mass Balance Closure Check card) draws the five major streams into and out of the kiln system — dry raw meal, fuel, combustion air, process water and feed moisture in; clinker, calcination CO₂, combustion CO₂, water vapour and unreacted air out — as live arrows sized only by the number next to them (not to visual scale), color-matched to the same convention used in the charts above (mineral = calcination/raw meal, fire = combustion/fuel). It's a simplified view: minor streams (water discharge, fuel ash to dust/bypass, raw-meal bypass dust) are left off the diagram for clarity and only appear in the Mass Balance Closure Check card's full audited breakdown directly below it.
Strict Audit Mode (Background Parameters & Emission Factors panel) — every upstream/background emission factor is tagged by provenance: green means it matches a logged source (e.g. Petcoke's or the grid factor's SimaPro export figure), amber means it needs attention — either a non-zero value with no recorded source (a placeholder, or a figure you've typed in yourself) or an unverified 0 with no data extracted for it yet. The console never substitutes a guessed industry-average number for a missing factor; it leaves it at 0 and flags it instead, so every downstream total stays traceable to either real data or a visibly marked gap.
Formulas follow IPCC 2006 Guidelines Vol. 3 Ch. 2 (Tier 2/3 mass-balance) and GCCA/CSI CO₂ & Energy Protocol conventions.
